## 🌟 Overview
**Jurassic Dino Run** is an immersive 3D prehistoric dinosaur riding and exploration simulator. Explore vast prehistoric worlds, approach and mount 9 distinct dinosaur species with custom saddles, dismount to explore on foot, gallop at high speeds, leap over obstacles, swim across primeval rivers, soar through the skies, collect glowing amber crystals, and play multiplayer with friends!

---

## 🎮 Key Features

- **🏇 True 3D Dinosaur Riding Mechanic**:
  - Approach any dinosaur and press `E` (or tap `MOUNT 🏇` on mobile) to climb into the saddle.
  - Full player synchronization with custom mount anchors tailored to each dinosaur's anatomy.
  - Dismount anytime to explore ancient valleys on foot.
- **🦖 9 Articulated Dinosaur Species**:
  1. **Tyrannosaurus Rex** — Massive apex predator with heavy ground-shaking footstep audio and fearsome roars.
  2. **Spinosaurus** — Semi-aquatic giant with a magnificent dorsal sail and **river swimming capabilities**.
  3. **Stegosaurus** — Armored quad with dual rows of diamond plates and spiked thagomizer tail.
  4. **Diplodocus** — Colossal long-necked sauropod with high-vantage rider saddle and sweeping strides.
  5. **Coelophysis** — Agile, slender bipedal scout with rapid acceleration.
  6. **Velociraptor** — Fast, lethal predator with feathered crests and high leaps.
  7. **Ankylosaurus** — Living tank fortress with bony carapace armor and tail club.
  8. **Triceratops** — Three facial horns, solid bone frill, and defensive frontal charge.
  9. **Pteranodon** — Aerodynamic crested flyer capable of **soaring flight**!
- **🗺️ 5 Prehistoric Biomes with Real 3D Terrain**:
  - **Jurassic Jungle**: Towering cycads, giant ferns, and winding rivers.
  - **Volcanic Wasteland**: Basalt crags, rising ash/embers, and hazardous molten lava streams.
  - **Golden Desert Valley**: Sweeping amber dunes and colossal fossil ribcages.
  - **Frozen Prehistoric Highlands**: Glacial snowfields, ice spires, and swirling blizzards.
  - **Ancient Swamp**: Murky peat wetlands with giant bioluminescent mushrooms.
- **🎨 Claymorphism UI Redesign**:
  - Tactile, puffy cards with soft ambient drop shadows and inset highlights.
  - Organic prehistoric palette: Warm Ivory, Soft Sand, Jungle Green, Volcanic Orange, and Sky Blue.
- **🌐 Real-Time Multiplayer Safari**:
  - Create or join rooms using 4-character codes (e.g. `JUR88`).
  - Synchronized player riding, dinosaur selection, roars, and 3D nametags.
- **🔊 Procedural 3D Web Audio Sound Engine**:
  - Footstep thuds modulated by dinosaur mass, vocal fold formant roars, ambient winds, and lava rumbles.
- **🕹️ Classic 2D Arcade Runner (Preserved)**:
  - Play the nostalgic 2D obstacle jumping race anytime from Mode Select.

## 🕹️ Controls Guide

### Desktop Keyboard & Mouse
| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `W` / `A` / `S` / `D` or Arrows | Walk / Steer Dinosaur or Explorer |
| `Left Shift` | Sprint / Gallop (Uses Stamina) |
| `Spacebar` | Jump (or Launch Flight with Pteranodon) |
| `E` | Mount Dinosaur / Dismount to explore on foot |
| `R` | Fearsome Species Roar |
| `Mouse Drag` | Orbit & Rotate 360° Camera View |
| `Mouse Scroll` | Zoom Camera in / out |
| `Escape` | Pause Simulation |

### Mobile Touchscreen Controls
- **Virtual Joystick (Bottom Left)**: Fluid 360° directional steering.
- **Touch Camera Drag**: Swipe anywhere across the screen to rotate the camera.
- **Tactile Clay Action Buttons (Bottom Right)**:
  - `🏇 MOUNT / DISMOUNT`: Context-sensitive mount into saddle or step down.
  - `▲ JUMP`: Leap over obstacles.
  - `⚡ SPRINT`: High-speed gallop.
  - `🗣️ ROAR`: Unleash species-specific roar.
